About Us

About Us Welcome to Booked Solid, your local independent bookstore with the convenience of nationwide book delivery. We’re proud to offer the warm, personal experience of a physical bookshop while also making it easy for customers to browse, order, and enjoy books from anywhere. At our store, you’ll find a carefully curated selection of fiction, non-fiction, children’s books, bestsellers, classics, and new releases. Our friendly, knowledgeable team is always here to recommend titles and help you discover your next favourite read. To make book shopping even easier, we created the Booked Solid Progressive Web App (PWA). Our PWA lets you explore our catalogue, check availability, reserve books, and place orders for fast home delivery—all from your phone, tablet, or computer. What We Offer • A welcoming in-store browsing experience • A wide range of genres for every type of reader • Expert recommendations from staff • Convenient book shipping straight to your door • A fast, mobile-friendly PWA for browsing and ordering At Booked Solid, we’re passionate about supporting our community of readers—both in person and online. Whether you visit us in-store or shop through our PWA, we’re committed to delivering great books, great service, and a great experience. Thank you for choosing Booked Solid—your trusted local bookstore and online bookshop.
